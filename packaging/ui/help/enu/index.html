Overview

Your storage at a glance

Use We.Rock.DiskMap to find large files, explore directories and reopen recorded results without scanning each time.

Three panes, one snapshot

Choose NAS, volume, share or subfolder on the left. The map includes deeper descendants of that scope. The right list shows its direct children by default.

Your first map in three steps

  1. Sign in as a DSM administrator and open We.Rock.DiskMap.
  2. If no shares are accessible, follow the read-permission guide. “Check shared folders again” refreshes inventory only; it starts no scan.
  3. Choose a share and click Scan. Use Scan NAS for all available scopes.

Open help

The question mark in the DSM window opens this local guide. Expand a topic on the left and select a section. All text and illustrations are bundled; reading requires no internet connection.
