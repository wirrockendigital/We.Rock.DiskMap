Scanning & refreshing

Scanning and the queue

A scan records file names, directories and sizes. Scanning leaves your source files unchanged.

Choose a scope

  • Scan: records a scope that has not been scanned.
  • Refresh: fully reads the selected scope again and publishes a new snapshot.
  • Scan NAS: scans all NAS scopes accessible to the package account.
  • Right-click the folder tree → Rescan folder: refreshes that scope.

Keep browsing while a scan runs

Changing the displayed folder does not stop a scan. You can keep viewing existing results. Another scan request enters the queue. Jobs run sequentially; a new request does not replace the active scan.

The queue icon in the header shows the active job and waiting scopes. The X beside a waiting item removes only that job. Cancel stops the active scan.

Understand progress

File traversal and index preparation are separate phases. Entry counts may stay unchanged during preparation. The ring can show a meaningful percentage only when a suitable total is known; otherwise it shows the size recorded so far.

Available previews are provisional and may change. A traversed folder is not automatically a separately completed scan while the overall job is running. Completed results are displayed; relevant notifications remain under the bell.

Scanning & refreshing

Scan age and partial coverage

Age and completeness answer different questions: when was the scope read, and could all relevant content be recorded?

Green, yellow, red or gray

The age indicator stays in the header when the tree is hidden. Tree dots also indicate age, progressing from green through yellow to red. Gray indicates a missing or unknown scan time. Set the age threshold under Settings → Scans in minutes, hours, days, weeks or months. A month means 30 days here.

Understand partial coverage

A recent scan can still contain gaps caused by missing permissions, changed or missing sources, or scan boundaries. Open Partial coverage to inspect specific reasons. Available partial results are usable, but do not prove completeness.

Intentionally excluded or hidden special content must be distinguished from genuine gaps. Hiding content does not repair permissions; the visible view uses the relevant coverage information.

Reuse recorded results

The app persists scan results. Opening it or navigating does not automatically start a full scan. Refresh is an explicit decision to read the scope again. A failed or cancelled job must not be treated as a successful new snapshot.
